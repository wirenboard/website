---
title: Warranties
---

Our warranty extends only to the original purchaser of the product and lasts for two years from the date of original purchase.

If you have an issue with our product, please provide a detailed description of your problem and e-mail us at [info@wirenboard.com](mailto:info@wirenboard.com) our engineers will provide the solution.

If you need repair, the manager will send you an act of defects. Please fill in all the fields and send us the electronic version of the act, and pack the original with the device.

In case of warranty, if we have a similar device in stock, we will send you a replacement device. The courier will bring a new one and pick up the faulty one. Pack the device securely when the courier arrives.

The repair period is up to 15 working days.

Refunds are possible due to manufacturing defects, in other cases, applications for a refund are accepted up to two weeks after receiving your order.
