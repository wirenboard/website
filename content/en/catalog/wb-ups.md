::product
#description

Uninterruptible power supply module on lithium polymer batteries. It is used to maintain the operation of the controller and connected modules in case of power loss. It has two modes of operation-12/24V, the mode is selected by a switch.


#info

## Technical parameters

::product-section{title="2 modes of operation"}
- 12 В
- 24 В
- The mode is selected by a switch
::

::product-section{title="Operating characteristics"}
- Operating temperature-charge: 0°C to 45°C, discharge: -20°C to 60°C
- Storage temperature: -5°C to 35°C
- DIN rail housing dimensions: 18.1 x 90.2 x 57.5 mm
- Two-color status indicator
::
