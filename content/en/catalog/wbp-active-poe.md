#description

The WBP-ACTIVE-POE power supply module is a built-in PoE 802.3af, 802.3at splitter with galvanic isolation for [Wiren Board 7.4](/product/wiren-board-74/) and Wiren Board 8.x controllers. Not suitable for the Wiren Board 8M in a metal case.

Supports 802.3af, 802.3a active PoE and 42V passive PoE.
