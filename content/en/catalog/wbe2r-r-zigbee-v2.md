#description

Adds a Zigbee interface. It is installed in the connector on the controller board. For Wiren Board 6…8.

Wi-Fi [antenna](https://wirenboard.com/en/product/wi-fi-2dbi-antenna/) is included, connection is made through the RP-SMA female connector.

Zigbee is a wireless communication standard as Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, created specifically for the Internet of things: from home automation (control of light, household appliances, audio-video equipment) to the construction of monitoring systems for large industrial facilities (temperature control and vibration of devices, collection of readings of electricity, water and gas consumption sensors, survey of fire and security sensors, etc.). Zigbee is used by all large companies in the smart home market.
