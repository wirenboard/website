#description

Built-in uninterruptible power supply on ionistors for Wiren Board versions **6.3-6.6.** The module powers the controller itself, the expansion and I / O modules, and external devices connected via 5Vout, Out and USB terminals.

The WBMZ2-SUPERCAP is installed on the second floor in the controller housing and connected by a cable to the internal connector.

**The module has a power** and operating time limit (2-3 minutes).
