#description

Built-in supercapacitor-based uninterruptible power supply for **Wiren Board 7…8.4**.

The module powers the controller itself, expansion and I / O modules, and external devices connected via the 5Vout, Vout and USB terminals.

The WBMZ4-SUPERCAP is installed in the controller case in the slot for the redundant power modules.

The module is limited in power and operating time (2-3 minutes).


::
