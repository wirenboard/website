#description

Built-in supercapacitor-based uninterruptible power supply for **[WBPRO-PLC](https://wirenboard.com/en/product/WBPRO-PLC7-2G-64G/)**. The module powers the controller itself, expansion and I / O modules, and external devices connected via the 5Vout, Vout and USB terminals.

The WBPRO-PMOD-ELDC is installed in the controller case in the slot for the redundant power modules.

**The module is limited in power** and operating time (2-3 minutes).


::
