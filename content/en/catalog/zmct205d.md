#description
One solid core current transformer for use with WB-MAP series of energy meters.

Measured current from 0.06 to 15 A.

#info
::product-section{title="Technical specifications"}
:photo{
  src="zmct205d/zmct205d_current_en.png"
  width="500px"
  float="right"
}
- Measured current from 0.06 to 15 A.
- Rated primary current 6 A.
- Accuracy class 0.5S.
- Transformation ratio 1:1000 (exact value on the label).
- Wire outer diameter up to 6.5 mm.
- Core type — solid.
- Core material — ferrite.
- Weight (not including wires) 3.7 g.
- Wire length 1.5 m.
