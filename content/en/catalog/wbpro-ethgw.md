#description

Model discontinued, recommended replacement [WBPRO-ETHGW3](/contents/product/WBPRO-ETHGW3)

The WBPRO-ETHGW bus coupler is designed for remote connection of Modbus RTU devices with RS-485 bus via Ethernet (Modbus TCP and Modbus RTU over TCP protocols). The module can be connected directly to the controller, as well as via local network or the Internet.
