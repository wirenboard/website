#description
One split core current transformer for use with WB-MAP series of energy meters.

Measured current from 0.25 to 152 A.

#info
::product-section{title="Technical specifications"}

- Measured current from 0.25 to 152 A.
- Rated primary current 25 A.
- Accuracy class 0.5S.
- Transformation ratio 1:3000 (exact value on the label).
- Wire outer diameter up to 16 mm.
- Core type — split.
- Core material — ferrite.
- Weight (not including wires) 90 g.
- Wire length 3 m.
