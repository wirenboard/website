::product
#description

Designed for controlling low-voltage loads and devices with dry contact inputs. No common ground with the controller is required.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Discrete outputs"}
- 8 outputs
- Each channel has galvanic isolation
- Normally open (SPST) contacts
- Current up to 400 mA per channel
- Voltage up to 30 V
::

::product-section{title="Interfaces"}
- WBIO bus
- Can be connected via Modbus RTU or Ethernet using the WB-MIO module
::

::product-section{title="Compatibility"}
- Wiren Board 5
- Wiren Board 6
- Wiren Board 7
- Wiren Board 8
- Interface modules WB-MIO and WB-MIO-E
::
