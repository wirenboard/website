Volatile organic compounds (VOCs) are substances released into the atmosphere in the form of gases: evaporation of varnishes/paints and elements of interior decoration (phenol, formaldehyde, toluene, styrene), alcohols, benzene, rotting vegetables, gases emitted by humans, household gas. High concentrations of hazardous VOCs pose a threat to human life and health.

The VOC sensor measures the total concentration of these substances with a typical error of ±15 %.
