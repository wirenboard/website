#description

Pulse counter module for connecting water, electricity, gas, and other types of meters with pulsed outputs of dry contact or open collector type.

When using momentary switches, the module can recognize up to four types of pressing and transmit events to the controller via Modbus.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Outputs"}
- No outputs on this device
::

::product-section{title="Discrete inputs"}
- 8 digital dry contact inputs
- Group galvanic isolation of inputs
- Pulse counter overflow protection
- Press detection: short, long, double and shortlong
- Pulse counting and input frequency measurement
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- Supply voltage: 9 to 28 VDC
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- DIN rail enclosure ( 36 x90 x57,5 mm)
::
