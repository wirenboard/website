#description

Universal tool for crimping almost the entire range of crimp bootlace ferrules


#info

## Technical parameters

::product-section{title="The range of sections of the ferrules:"}
- Insulated wire ferrules: 0.25–10 mm²
- Insulated wire twin ferrules: 2х0.5–2х6.0 mm²
::

::product-section{title="Additional functionality"}
- One self-regulating matrix for all range sizes
- The tool covers a range of 17 sizes of bushing tips
- Square crimping profile with fluting
- Crimp matrix width: 13 mm
- Regulator clamping force matrices
- An improved ratchet mechanism assures full cycle crimping
- Two-component ergonomic handles with small opening
::
