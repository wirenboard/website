#description
An industrial 64 GB microSD memory card made by SCY (Shenzhen Shichuangyi Electronics) for controllers and other devices with a microSD slot.

U3 and V30 speed classes, operating temperature from -25 to +85 °C, 3,000 program/erase cycles.

#info
::product-section{title="Technical specifications"}
:photo{
  src="microsd-industrial-64gb/microsd-industrial-64gb_front.png"
  width="250px"
  float="right"
}
- Manufacturer SCY (Shenzhen Shichuangyi Electronics), part number STS0MBB064G00.
- Capacity 64 GB, 58.8 GB available to the user.
- Speed classes: Class 10, UHS Speed Class 3 (U3), Video Speed Class 30 (V30).
- UHS-I interface, bus modes up to SDR104 — up to 104 MB/s.
- Sequential read up to 93 MB/s, write up to 88 MB/s.
- Complies with SD specification version 6.1.
- Supply voltage 2.7…3.6 V.
- Endurance 3,000 program/erase (P/E) cycles.
- Global wear leveling and ECC error correction.
- Dimensions 15 × 11 × 1.0 mm, weight 0.33 g max.
::

::product-section{title="Reliability and operating conditions"}
- Operating temperature from -25 to +85 °C.
- Storage temperature from -40 to +85 °C.
- Humidity: 95 % at 25 °C in operation, 93 % at 40 °C for 500 hours in storage.
- Salt water spray: 3 % NaCl at 35 °C for 24 hours, per MIL-STD method 1009.
- Drop test from 1.5 m.
- Bending 10 N, torque 0.10 N·m at up to 2.5° deflection.
- Electrostatic discharge per IEC 61000-4-2: contact ±2 and ±4 kV, air up to ±15 kV.
- Compliant with RoHS and REACH.
::
