#description

Wiren Board 6 Zero is a controller based on the newest Wiren Board 6 platform with an optimized set of ports and interfaces. It comes with a preinstalled iRidium server (60 day trial) and a basic Wiren Board Web interface (free of charge).



Iridium server is a platform for smart home and IoT controllers known to developers in 25 countries for its convenience in design, configuration flexibility and ease of use.


You can use it to tailor the interface to manage both smart home and smart office from portable and stationary devices, configure remote control and scenarios.



[Examples of iRidium projects.](http://www.iridiummobile.ru/projects/ready-solutions/)
