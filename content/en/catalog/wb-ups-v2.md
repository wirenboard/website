#description

**The device is discontinued, [a new version](/en/product/wb-ups-v3/) is available.**

Li-polymer battery backup module. Maintains an output voltage of 12 or 24V in the absence of input power.

Batteries are already integrated into the device's housing.

Suitable for equipment from other manufacturers.


#info

## Technical Specifications

::product-section{title="Technical Specifications"}
- Input Voltage: 12-28 V
- Output Voltage: 12 V / 24 V (selected by switch)
- Power: 15 W
- Operating Time up to 60 minutes (at 10 W)
::

::product-section{title="Operating Characteristics"}
- Operating Temperature - charge: from 0°C to 55°C, discharge: from -20°C to 60°C
- Storage Temperature: from -5°C to 35°C
- Status Indicator
- DIN Rail Mount, 2 modules
- Dimensions: 36x90x58 mm
::
