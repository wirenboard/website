#description

The module has been discontinued. Recommended replacement - [WB-CT309 v.2](/product/wb-ct309-v2/). Сurrent transformer strip with three solid-core current transformers for use with WB-MAP energy meters. CTs are arranged in 1 DIN module increments. Sold with an adapter from RJ-12 to terminal block, 70 cm cable and spare RJ-12 plugs.


#info

::product-section{title="Technical specifications"}
- Rated current 5 А
- Maximum current 125 А
- Accuracy class 0,5S
- Opening diameter 9.5 mm
- Dimensions 68\*37\*30 mm
- Weight 70 g
