#description

**Recommended Replacement [WB-REF-U](https://wirenboard.com/product/WB-REF-U/)**

Network card for Carel refrigeration controller, which adds an RS-485 interface with Modbus RTU protocol support. The device is connected to the Carel controller through a standard connector. Flex cable included.

WB-REF-U-CR can be used instead of original network card _IROPZ48505 (IROPZ48500)_ to connect Carel temperature controller to controller [Wiren Board](/product/wiren-board-6/).

Suitable for the families of controllers Carel BASIC and Carel EASY, not a complete list of models:
