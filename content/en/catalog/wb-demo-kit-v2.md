#description

WB-demo-kit is an assembled and programmed set of the equipment for demonstration to the customers or for independent fast mastering of Wiren Board devices.

The demo-kit equipment and programmed rules show common usage scenarios of Wiren Board devices:

- indoor air quality control
- climate control
- monitoring of flooding in inhabited and production locations
- polling of water and electric power meters
- lightning control
- multi-circuit electricity metering
- equipment failure warning and prevention in some cases
- stable system operation in case of power outage or loss

WB-demo-kit is delivered with detailed documentation: complete diagram of connection, power supply diagram, detailed description of scenarios.

Prices are indicated on a turnkey basis.
