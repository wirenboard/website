#description

Built-in supercapacitor-based uninterruptible power supply for **Wiren Board 6.7**. The module powers the controller itself, expansion and I / O modules, and external devices connected via the 5Vout, Vout and USB terminals. The WBMZ3-SUPERCAP is installed in the controller case in the slot for the redundant power modules. The module is limited in power and operating time (2-3 minutes).


::
