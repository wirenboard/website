#description

License for Sprut.hub software and a module for connecting Zigbee devices. Works only with Sprut.hub home automation software.

Installed in a connector on the controller board, suitable for Wiren Board 6…8.

Wi-Fi [antenna](https://wirenboard.com/en/product/wi-fi-2dbi-antenna/) is included, connection is made through the SMA male connector
