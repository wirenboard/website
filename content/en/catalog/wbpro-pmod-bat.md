#description

Built-in uninterruptible power supply for WBPRO-PLC for **[WBPRO-PLC](https://wirenboard.com/en/product/wiren-board-7/)**. The module powers the controller itself, the expansion and modules, and external devices connected via the 5Vout, Vout and USB terminals.

The WBPRO-PMOD-BAT is installed in the slot for backup power modules in the controller housing.

**The module has a power limit** and is designed for small installations.
