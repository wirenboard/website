::product
#description

A set of 6 voltage dividers for [WB-MAI6](https://wirenboard.com/product/wb-mai6/).

Allows to measure DC voltage up to 60 V with input impedance of 150 kOhm


#info

::product-section{title="Technical specifications"}
- Voltage division coefficient 16.
- Input impedance > 150 kOhm.
- Input voltage up to 60 V.
- Two channels.
- Designed to be installed in the terminal block of WB-MAI6.
