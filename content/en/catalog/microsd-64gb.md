#description
An industrial 64 GB microSD memory card made by SCY (Shenzhen Shichuangyi Electronics). For controllers and other devices with a microSD slot.

Operates from -25 to +85 °C — in electrical cabinets and unheated rooms. Wear leveling and ECC error correction extend the service life under continuous writing of logs, history and backups.

#info
::product-section{title="Technical specifications"}
:photo{
  src="microsd-64gb/microsd-64gb_specs_en.png"
  width="500px"
  float="right"
}
- Capacity: 64 GB.
- Read speed: up to 93 MB/s.
- Write speed: up to 88 MB/s.
- Speed classes: Class 10, UHS Speed Class 3 (U3), Video Speed Class 30 (V30).
- Operating temperature: from -25 to +85 °C.
- Storage temperature: from -40 to +85 °C.
::

::product-section{title="Reliability and endurance"}
:photo{
  src="microsd-64gb/microsd-64gb_wear_leveling_en.png"
  width="500px"
  float="right"
}
- Global wear leveling across the entire memory.
- ECC error correction.
- Endurance: 3000 program/erase (P/E) cycles.
- Operating humidity: 95 % at 25 °C.
- Electrostatic discharge immunity per IEC 61000-4-2: contact up to ±4 kV, air up to ±15 kV.
- Withstands a drop from a height of 1.5 m.
::

::product-section{title="Other"}
- Manufacturer: SCY (Shenzhen Shichuangyi Electronics).
- Complies with SD specification version 6.1.
- RoHS and REACH compliant.
