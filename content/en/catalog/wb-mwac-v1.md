#description

**Discontinued. Recommended replacement - [WB-MWAC v.2](/product/WB-MWAC/)**

Everything you need for the typical task of monitoring the wet core of the apartment:

- water leak detection,
- automatic control of shut-off valves,
- counting pulses from water meters even in case of power loss

The device works with the Fast Modbus protocol extension — provides scanning capabilities, convenient configuration and instant polling of device status.


#info

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

::product-section{title="Configuration"}
- 3 channels for connection of sensors of leaks
- 3 channel switches for manual control
- 2 channels to connect pulsed water meters
- 2 channels to control the actuators of valves
- Water leaks protection system
::

::product-section{title="General characteristics"}
- Power supply: 9 — 28 VDC
- Control via RS-485, Modbus RTU Protocol
- 3DIN module width, mounting on DIN rail
- Supports active and passive leak sensors
- Compatible with controllers Wiren Board and other controllers via Modbus RTU
::

::product-section{title="Advantages"}
- Polls the pulses of counters even in case of power loss (up to 5 months)
- Direct control for relay channels, for the forced opening of the valves or run user scripts
- Quick response to the leak, regardless of the central controller
::
