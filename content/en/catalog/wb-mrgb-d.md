**This model has been discontinued. It has been replaced by the [WB-MRGBW-D](https://wirenboard.com/en/product/WB-MRGBW-D/) model. For gross purchases please contact us [info@wirenboard.com](mailto:info@contactless.ru).**

Three channel dimmer for managing LED strips. Can manage one RGB strip or three monochrome strips individually


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Outputs"}
- 3 channels, up to 3A per channel, 12-24 V
- No flicker effect, due to high PWM frequency (24 kHz)
- Saving state when the power is turned off
::

::product-section{title="Discrete inputs"}
- Two digital inputs:
- Short press-activate/deactivate all channels.
- Long button press — smooth color changing
- Group galvanic isolation of inputs
- "Dry contact" inputs
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- Supply voltage: 9 to 28 VDC
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- 2-module wide DIN rail enclosure (36 x 90 x 58 mm)
::
