Electrochemical gas detection sensor WB-MSGR with built-in relay. The sensor is a leak detection tool and is not suitable for accurate measurement of gas concentration.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Measured values"}
- Detection of presence of combustible gases (natural gas, propane, methylpropane, methane, hydrogen) or organic gases (NH3, NOx, alcohol, benzene, tobacco smoke, CO2)
::

::product-section{title="Outputs"}
- 1 relay channel rated 3A 230 VAC
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- Supply voltage: 9 — 24 VDC
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- Plastic enclosure with a DIN-rail mount and wall mounting holes (65x46x29 mm)
::
