#description

The 4-channel lighting control module is designed to control lighting fixtures equipped with 0-10V interfaces, PWM, as well as to manage arbitrary loads equipped with compatible inputs.

**Available upon request.**


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Outputs"}
- 4 outputs 0-10VAC
- Saving state when the power is turned off
::

::product-section{title="Discrete inputs"}
- Three digital inputs
- Group galvanic isolation of inputs
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- Supply voltage: 11 to 24 VDC
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- Dimensions: 65 x 35 x 7 mm
::
