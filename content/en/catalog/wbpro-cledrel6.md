::product
#description

Compact relay module with high in-rush current capacity for lighting management and switching general purpose loads.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Outputs"}
- 6 channels with relays rated 10A 230 VAC
- Channel status indication
- Maximum continuous current: 16 A per channel
- Maximum in-rush current: 800 A (200 µs)
::

::product-section{title="Discrete inputs"}
- no discrete inputs
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- Supply voltage: 9 to 28 VDC
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- 3-module wide DIN rail enclosure (53 x 90 x 58 mm)
::
