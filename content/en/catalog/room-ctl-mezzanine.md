Wiren Board 8 controller with a built-in Room Ctl module — a functional equivalent of WB-MR6C v.2 and WB-MAO4 inside the controller enclosure: 6 relays, 3 dry-contact inputs, 4 analog 0–10 V outputs.

Saves five units in the electrical panel. For residential complexes and hotels.
