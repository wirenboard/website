#description

Designed for controlling contactors, signal lamps, and other low-power loads.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Relay outputs"}
- 16 two-position, normally open outputs (SPST, NO);
- Two groups of 8 outputs with a common contact;
- Rated current of 1A per channel, but not more than 10A per group.
