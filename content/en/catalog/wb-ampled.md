::product
#description

Four-channel amplifier for dimming LED strips with a current of up to 10 A per channel.

Used in conjunction with the WB-LED dimmer or other LED strip controllers. Allows you to increase the maximum current of the dimmable channel.


#info

## Main characteristics

::product-section{title="Advantages"}
:photo{
  src="wb-ampled/wb-ampled-1.png"
  width="500px"
  float="right"
}

- 4 channels of 10A per channel.
- Tape voltage up to 48 V.
- Operating frequency up to 24 kHz, allows use with different tape drivers.
- Can be used with third party dimmers or controlled by buttons.
- Overheat protection.
::

::product-section{title="Hardware"}
:photo{
  src="wb-ampled/wb-ampled-2.png"
  width="500px"
  float="right"
}

Specifications:

- Maximum current: 10 A per channel.
- Allowable channel voltage: up to 48 V.
- Input type: “Dry contact”, common ground.
- Overheat protection response temperature: +90 °C.

- Service life: 5 years.
- Extended operating temperature range: -40…+80 °C.
- DIN rail enclosure, 2M (36 x 90 x 58 mm).
::

::product-section{title="Increasing dimming power"}
:photo{
  src="wb-ampled/wb-ampled-3.png"
  width="500px"
  float="right"
}

The WB-AMPLED amplifier is used when the dimmer power of the LED strip is insufficient and allows you to increase the current per channel up to 10 A. If this is not enough, then you can connect several amplifiers in parallel.

The total current of all 4 channels flows through the wires connected to the negative terminals, i.e. up to 40 A. This is a fairly large current, so it is important to correctly calculate the cross-sections of the supply wires.
::
