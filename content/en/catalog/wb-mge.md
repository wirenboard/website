#description

**The module has been discontinued. Recommended replacement [WB-MGE v.3](/contents/product/wb-mge-v3)**

The WB-MGE bus coupler is designed for remote connection of Modbus RTU devices with RS-485 bus via Ethernet (Modbus TCP and Modbus RTU over TCP protocols). The module can be connected directly to the controller, as well as via local network or the Internet.
