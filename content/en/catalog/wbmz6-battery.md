#description

Built-in uninterruptible power supply for **Wiren Board******8.5****. The module powers the controller itself, the expansion and modules, and external devices connected via the 5Vout, Vout and USB terminals.

The WBMZ6-BATTERY is installed in the slot for backup power modules in the controller housing.

**The module has a power limitation** and is intended for small installations. The operating time of the controller is from 50 minutes to 8.5 hours, depending on the load and the presence of a modem.
