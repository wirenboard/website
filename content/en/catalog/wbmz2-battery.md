#description

Built-in uninterruptible power supply for Wiren Board 6. The module powers the controller, its extension and I/O modules and external devices connected via 5Vout, Vout and USB terminals.

The WBMZ2-BATTERY is installed as a second level into the controller enclosure and attached via ribbon cable to an internal connector. A button for forced shutdown of the controller is provided on the module board.

**The module is limited in power** and is intended for small-scale systems.
