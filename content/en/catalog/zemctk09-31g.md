#description
One split core current transformer for use with WB-MAP series of energy meters.

Measured current from 5 to 755 A.

#info

- Measured current from 5 to 755 A.
- Rated primary current 500 A.
- Accuracy class 0.5S.
- Transformation ratio 1:10000 (exact value on the label).
- Wire outer diameter up to 35 mm.
- Core type — split.
- Core material — sheet carbon steel.
- Weight (not including wires) ~362 g.
- Wire length 3 m.
