#description

**Recommended replacement [ParLan Patch U/UTP Cat5e PVC 2x2x0.35 mm sq.](/product/cable-LAN-2/)**

The cable is used in low-cost installations for laying RS-485 networks instead of the indoor signal cable. One of the four twisted pairs is used for signals A and B, and the other two pairs are used for power.

The symmetrical ParLan Patch F/UTP Cat5e 4x2x0,60 PVC cable is used in digital telephony, Ethernet networks, as well as in video monitoring, alarm and ACS systems. The design contains 4 twisted pairs (multi-wire twisted pair conductors) in dense PE insulation with individual color coding according to international standards. The outer shell of PVC plastic fully meets the requirements of the PB. Resistant to electromagnetic influences and radio frequency interference.

The price is for 1m.
