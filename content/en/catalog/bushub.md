#description

Splitter for connecting the RS-485 bus “star” in the control cabinet. Can also be used for isolated ground bus, power supply, etc. Installed on DIN rail, detachable terminals.


#info

## Technical characteristics

::product-section{title="Terminals"}
- Quantity - 2 x 7 pcs.
- Voltage - up to 300V AC.
- Current - up to 8 A.
::

::product-section{title="Other"}
- DIN rail housing, 2M (35×90×36 mm).
::
