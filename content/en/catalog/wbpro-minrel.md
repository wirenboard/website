#description

Compact relay module. Installable in a junction box.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Outputs"}
- 2 relay channels
- Model versions:
  - /NO — NO contact relay rated 10А 230 VAC

  - /NC — NC contact relay rated 2А 230 VAC
::

::product-section{title="Discrete inputs"}
- 2 discrete inputs with flexible channel control configuration
- "Dry contact" inputs
- Inputs pulses count
- Inputs frequency measure
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- Supply voltage: 9 to 28 VDC
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- Small size: 52 x 22 x 20 mm
::
