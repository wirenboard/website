#description

Measures the concentration of volatile organic compounds (VOC) in the range from 0 to 1000 ppm. The sensor reacts to organic gases. These are varnish / paint vapors, alcohols, rotting vegetables, human gases, and also detects domestic gas leaks. It complements but does not exclude the CO2 sensor.


::
