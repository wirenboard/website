#description

eBus coupler WBE2-I-EBUS is designed to work as an extension module for the WirenBoard 6…8 controllers, and is used for remote control of gas and electric boilers with eBus interface.


#info

## Technical Specifications

::product-section{title="Power Specifications"}
- nominal supply voltage: 5 V;
- maximum current consumption: 0.5 A.
::

::product-section{title="RS-485 / Modbus settings"}
- baud rate: 19200 bps;
- number of stop bits: 1;
- parity: none;
- data bits: 8;
- default device address: 12;
- optimal device polling interval: once per second.
::

::product-section{title="Other"}
- dimensions: 39x26x9 mm;
- weight: 30 g.
::
