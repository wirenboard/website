#description

4-channel amplifier for dimming LED strips. Up to 10 A per channel. Can be used in conjunction with

[WBPRO-LEDDIM](https://wirenboard.com/en/product/WBPRO-LEDDIM/)dimmer or other LED strip controllers.


#info

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

::product-section{title="Outputs"}
- 4 channels, 10 A each, up to 48 V
::

::product-section{title="Inputs"}
- 4 inputs for control signals
- Working frequency up to 24kHz
::

::product-section{title="Other"}
- Built-in overheating protection
- 2-module wide DIN rail mounted enclosure 36 x 90 x 58 mm
::
