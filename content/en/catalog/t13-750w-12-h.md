#description

Frequency converter for connecting AC motors up to 750 W. Mains supply 220 V. Output for 3 phases. Interface RS-485, Modbus RTU.

Third party hardware. The device has features of working under the Modbus RTU protocol, which leads to exchange errors, therefore, in the web interface of the Wiren Board controller, the device channels may be colored red for a short time. This does not affect the control of the device and its functions.

Made in China.


#info

## Technical Specifications

::product-section{title="Input"}
- Power supply: 230 VAC
::

::product-section{title="Output"}
- Power of the connected electric motor: <= 750 W
- The range of possible change of the output frequency: 1.0 - 99.0 Hz
- PWM carrier frequency: 8 kHz
- Maximum output current: 3.86A
::

::product-section{title="Other"}
- Internal power consumption: 20 W or less
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- Degree of moisture and dust protection: IP20
- Dimensions: 130 x 60 x 100 mm
- Net weight: 0.75 kg
::
