#description

120 Ohm resistor in a convenient housing with a terminal block. Installed at the ends of the RS-485 line to suppress reflections.


#info

## Specifications

::product-section{title="Common"}
- Resistance: 118 Ohm +-5%.
- Maximum cross-section of clamped wire: 2.5 sq. mm.
- Dimensions: 12\*17\*35 mm.
- Weight: 10 g.
