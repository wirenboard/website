#description

Designed for managing low voltage loads, devices with dry contact inputs. The devices do not require common ground with the controller.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Discrete outputs"}
- 8 outputs
- Galvanic isolation per channel
- Normally open (SPST) contacts
- Current up to 400 mA per channel
- Voltage up to 30V
