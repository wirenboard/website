#description

Built-in uninterruptible power supply for **Wiren Board******6.7–6.9****. The module powers the controller itself, the expansion and modules, and external devices connected via the 5Vout, Vout and USB terminals.

The WBMZ3-BATTERY is installed in the slot for backup power modules in the controller housing.

**The module has a power limit** and is designed for small installations. Controller operating time up to 13 hours.


::
