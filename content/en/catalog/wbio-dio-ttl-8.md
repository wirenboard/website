#description

**This model is deprecated. Do not place it in your projects.**
Designed for connection of devices with 5V TTL or 5V CMOS signals. Consists of 8 I/O 5V voltage channels configured as output by default


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Outputs"}
- 8 channels of 5V GPIO type
- Group galvanic isolation
::

::product-section{title="Interfaces"}
- WBIO Bus
- Can be connected to Modbus RTU and Ethernet by via the WB-MIO module
::

::product-section{title="Compatibility"}
- Wiren Board 5 controllers
- Wiren Board 6 controllers
- Bus couplers WB-MIO and WB-MIO-E
::
