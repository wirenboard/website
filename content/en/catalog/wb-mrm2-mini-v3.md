#description

**The device is currently under development, so stay tuned for updates.**

Designed for switching general-purpose loads up to 3.2 kW, including inductive loads: heaters, lighting, curtain motors, etc.

A compact, flush-mounted module with thermostat mode. Equipped with universal inputs for buttons, limit switches, and 1-Wire digital temperature sensors. Supports automatic heater control based on set thresholds and hysteresis, based on sensor data.

Rated current: 16 A, inrush current: up to 400 A (1.5 ms).
 2 relay outputs, 4 1-Wire/DI/DO inputs.
