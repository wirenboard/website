Model discontinued. Recommended replacement: [WBC-4G v.2](/ru/product/wbc-4g-v2/).

3G modem. Installed into the Wiren Board 6.7 controller. The kit includes a right-angle antenna with an SMA connector.

## Technical Specifications

::product-section{title="General Specifications"}

- SMS messaging.
- Support for two nano-SIM cards.
- Alternating operation mode.
- Antenna included
