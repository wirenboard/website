#description
One split core current transformer for use with WB-MAP series of energy meters.

Measured current from 0.2 to 27 A.

#info
::product-section{title="Technical specifications"}
:photo{
  src="ct-heyi-kct-06/ct-heyi-kct-06_current_en.png"
  width="500px"
  float="right"
}
- Measured current from 0.2 to 27 A.
- Rated primary current 20 A.
- Accuracy class 0.5S.
- Transformation ratio 1:2000 (exact value on the label).
- Wire outer diameter up to 5.5 mm.
- Core type — split.
- Core material — ferrite.
- Weight (not including wires) 15 g.
- Wire length 3 m.
