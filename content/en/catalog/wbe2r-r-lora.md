#description

An extension module for Wiren Board controllers that organizes wireless communication between the controller and Wiren Board LoRa devices.

Not suitable for devices with LoRaWan.


#info

## Specifications

::product-section{title="Connection"}
- Wireless, 869.125 MHz
- Antenna included<
::

::product-section{title="Connected Devices"}
- [WB-MSW-LORA v.4 universal sensor](https://wirenboard.com/product/wb-msw-lora-v4/)
::

::product-section{title="Compatibility"}
- Wiren Board 6…8 controllers
