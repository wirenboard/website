#description

The WBPRO-METER6 energy meter is perfect for consumption analysis in apartments and private homes. It is designed for operation in single phase AC networks.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Measurements"}
- 6 single phase consumers
- Measures instantaneous parameters: voltage, current, power
- Stores accumulated values of energies
- Measurement of active, reactive, full power and energy, power factor
::

::product-section{title="Connection"}
- Connection via split type current transformers
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- Supply voltage: 9 to 28 VDC
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- 3-module wide DIN rail mounted enclosure
::
