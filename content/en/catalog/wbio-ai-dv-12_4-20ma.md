#description

Designed for measuring 4-20 mA analog signals. A typical use for this module is retrieving data from sensors with current output: thermometers, pressure sensors, etc.



**Warning: only one such module can be connected to the controller!**


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Analog inputs"}
- 12 single-ended 4-20 mA inputs
- Group galvanic isolation
::

::product-section{title="Interfaces"}
- WBIO Bus
- Can be connected to Modbus RTU and Ethernet by means of the WB-MIO module
::

::product-section{title="Compatibility"}
- Wiren Board 5 controllers
- Wiren Board 6 controllers
- Wiren Board 7 controllers
- Bus couplers WB-MIO and WB-MIO-E
::
