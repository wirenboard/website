#description

Insulated twin wire ferrules are designed for mounting on the ends of stranded copper wires for the formation of the integrity at the end of the wire when connected to the contact sockets of the electrical equipment. Modern solution when connected to the terminals of electrical appliances, replaces the laborious process of maintenance or twisting stranded copper wires.
