#description

The module powers the controller itself, its extension and I/O modules and external devices connected via 5Vout and USB terminals. The WBMZ-BATTERY is installed as a second level into the controller enclosure and attached via ribbon cable to an internal connector. When the module is ordered together with the Wiren Board 5 controller as a set, the controller comes equipped with a shutdown button on the front panel. When the module is purchased separately, a controller front panel with the forced shutdown button is included in the delivery set.
