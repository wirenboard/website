#description

Uninterruptible power supply module on lithium-polymer batteries. Supports an output voltage of 12 or 24V when there is no input power. The rechargeable batteries are built into the body of the device. Suitable for equipment from other manufacturers.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Specifications"}
- Input voltage: 10–27 V;
- Output voltage: 9–25 V (when powered from battery);
- Power: 15 W, up to 20 W peak;
- Operating time: up to 60 min (at 15 W);
- Power consumption during charging: up to 6 W;
::

::product-section{title="Performance"}
- Operating temperature — during charge: 0°C to 45°C, during discharge: -20°C to 60°C;
- Recommended storage temperature: -5°C to 35°C;
- Status indicator;
- DIN rail mount, 2 modules;
- Dimensions: 36×90×58 mm;
::
