#description

2G modem. Installed in the Wiren Board 6.7 controller. The set includes an angular antenna with an SMA connector.


#info

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

::product-section{title="General"}
- SIM800C-DS modem from SIMCom
- Sending SMS messages
- Support for two SIM-cards nano-SIM
- Alternating SIM operating mode
- Antenna included.
::

::product-section{title="Compatibility"}
- Wiren Board 6.7 and higher controllers
