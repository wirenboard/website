#description
One split core current transformer for use with WB-MAP series of energy meters.

Measured current from 0.35 to 290 A.

#info

- Measured current from 0.35 to 290 A.
- Rated primary current 35 A.
- Accuracy class 0.5S.
- Transformation ratio 1:5000 (exact value on the label).
- Wire outer diameter up to 24 mm.
- Core type — split.
- Core material — ferrite.
- Weight (not including wires) 190 g.
- Wire length 2 m.
