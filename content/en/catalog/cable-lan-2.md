#description

Model discontinued. Recommended replacement: [WB-CABLE v.1 2x2x0.35 mm²](https://wirenboard.com/product/wb-cable_v1/)

This is a flexible (stranded) twisted-pair cable. It has 2 pairs of conductors with a diameter of 0.78 mm (cross-section 0.35 mm2, 22 AWG), Category 5e.

The cable is used in budget installations for laying RS-485 networks. One pair is used for A and B signals, and the other for power supply. The stranded pairs are convenient to crimp with ferrules (bootlace terminals).

The price is per meter.


#info

## Technical Specifications

::product-section{title="Construction"}
- Stranded copper conductors
- Solid polyethylene core insulation
- Twisted pairs
- Unshielded
- PVC sheath
::

::product-section{title="Parameters"}
- Number of cores: 4 (2 pairs)
- Conductor diameter: 0.78 mm
- Conductor gauge: 22 AWG
- Conductor cross-section: 0.35 mm2
- Cable diameter: 6.0 mm
- Weight per 1 m of cable: 41 g
::

::product-section{title="Electrical parameters"}
- DC loop resistance: max. 12 Ω/100 m
- Pair capacitance: max. 5.6 nF/100 m (at 0.8–1 kHz)
- Nominal characteristic impedance: 100 Ω
::
