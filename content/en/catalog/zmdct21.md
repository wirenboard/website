#description
One solid core current transformer for use with WB-MAP series of energy meters.

Measured current from 0.8 to 140 A.

#info
::product-section{title="Technical specifications"}
:photo{
  src="zmdct21/zmdct21_current_en.png"
  width="500px"
  float="right"
}
- Measured current from 0.8 to 140 A.
- Rated primary current 80 A.
- Accuracy class 0.2S.
- Transformation ratio 1:2500 (exact value on the label).
- Wire outer diameter up to 12 mm.
- Core type — solid.
- Core material — ferrite.
- Weight (not including wires) 48.6 g.
- Wire length 1.5 m.
