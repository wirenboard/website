::product
#description

Low-cost power supply on DIN-rail. Adjustable output voltage


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Input"}
- Input voltage 85 ... 264VAC.
- Input/output insulation voltage: 3000 V.
::

::product-section{title="Output"}
- Output voltage adjustable in the range of 12-24V.
- Output current 1 A (at 12V), 0.5 A (at 24V).
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- 2-module wide DIN rail enclosure (35 × 90 × 54.5 mm)
- !!! ~ 5 mm taller than other devices when mounted on a DIN rail. This module stands above the front panel of some switchboard enclosures.
::
