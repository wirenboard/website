#description

Designed for measuring values of analog signals. Typical uses of this module include acquisition of data from analog thermometers, measurement of voltage at individual elements of a battery, etc.



**Warning: only one such module can be connected to the controller!**


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Analog inputs"}
- 12 single-ended or
- 6 differential inputs
- Group galvanic isolation
- Two operation modes: "± 50V" and "0-2.5V"
