#description

Electricity meter WBPRO-METER3T is designed for energy management and power quality monitoring. The WBPRO-METER3T can be used in apartment buildings, data centers and smart offices. Designed for operation in single- or three-phase AC networks. The load is connected by passing a wire through a solid-core transformer. This meter is well suited for operation in conjunction with current transformers with a secondary current of 5A. The WBPRO-METER3T can also detect voltage surges and measure inrush currents of loads such as lighting or motors.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Measurements"}
- One three phase or three single phase consumers
- Maximum current is 125 A
- Rated current is 5 A
- Accuracy class 0,5 S (<0.5% error)
- Measures instantaneous parameters: voltage, current, power
- Stores accumulated values of energies
- Individual values on all 3 phases and totals for the three-phase load
- Measurement of active, reactive, full power and energy
- Power factor, voltage and current angles
- Peak values of current and voltage
::

::product-section{title="Connection"}
- By passing a wire through a solid-core current transformer
- Wire diameter up to 9.5 mm
- Detection of incorrect phasing
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- Supply voltage: 9 to 28 VDC
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- 3-module wide DIN rail mounted enclosure (58 x 90 x 75 mm)
::
