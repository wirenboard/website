#description

USB<->RS-485 interface converter with 12V (400mA) power supply to control devices via RS-485 bus from a computer, laptop, tablet or Linux PLC.

WB-USB485 can be used to configure and update the firmware of Wiren Board Modbus devices. Supports any protocol that uses the RS-485 bus.

The device has three indicators: overcurrent (Vout\_OK) and exchange on the RS-485 bus (RX, TX).

No driver installation required on latest versions of Windows, Linux, MacOS and Android.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Outputs"}
- RS-485: A/B without galvanic isolation
- 12V up to 400mA
- Short circuit protection
