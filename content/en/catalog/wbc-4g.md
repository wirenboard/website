## Technical Specifications

:include{path="/catalog/includes/modem_4g_general"}

::product-section{title="Compatibility"}
- Wiren Board 6.7–6.9 controllers
::

::product-section{title="Frequencies and Data Rates"}
- Operating frequency bands
  - LTE-FDD B1, B3, B5, B7, B8, B20
  - LTE-TDD B38, B40
  - UMTS/HSPA 900/2100 MHz (B1, B8)
  - GSM/GPRS/EDGE 900/1800 MHz

- Data transfer rate
  - FDD-LTE Cat4 - up to 150 Mbps (download), up to 50 Mbps (upload)
  - TDD-LTE Cat4 - up to 130 Mbps (download), up to 35 Mbps (upload)
  - HSPA+ - up to 42 Mbps (download), up to 5.76 Mbps (upload)
  - WCDMA - up to 384 kbps (download), up to 384 kbps (upload)
  - EDGE - up to 236.8 kbps (download)
  - GPRS - up to 85.6 kbps (download), up to 85.6 kbps (upload)
::
