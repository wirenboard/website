::product
#description

Compact module for polling two remote digital 1-Wire temperature sensors over the RS-485 bus. Allows the use of 1-Wire sensors in conditions of strong interference (e.g. a high-energy power supply operating nearby) and at a considerable distance from the controller.


#info

## Technical specifications

::product-section{title="Measured values"}
- 2 inputs for external 1-Wire temperature sensors (DS18B20)
- 1 internal temperature sensor
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- Supply voltage: 9 to 28 VDC
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- Small size: 38 × 17 × 13 mm
::
