#description

This is a version of the KDVVGng(A)-LS 2x2x0.35 cable with a changed color marking of the cores - red/black and yellow/white. This is a flexible (multi-wire) cable twisted in pairs. It has 2 pairs of wires with a cross section of 0.35 mm2, 22 AWG.

The cable is used in budget installations for laying RS-485 networks. One of the pairs is used for signals A and B, the other for power. It is convenient to crimp multi-wire cable pairs into NShVI lugs.

The price is for one meter.


#info

::product-section{title="Construction"}
- Copper stranded conductors
- Insulation of cores made of solid polyethylene
- Pair twist
- No screen
- Sheath made of PVC plastic compound ng-LS - non-flammable, with reduced smoke emission
::

::product-section{title="Options"}
- Number of cores: 4 (2 pairs)
- Code gauge: 22 AWG
- Code cross-section: 0.35 mm2
- Code material: copper
- Cable diameter: 6.8 mm
- Weight of 1 m cable: 62 g
::

::product-section{title="Electrical parameters"}
- Characteristic impedance: 100 Ohm
- Frequency: 1 MHz
- Electrical resistance of the DC circuit: no more than 6 Ohm/100m
- The electrical capacitance of a pair is no more than 7.5 nF/100m (at a frequency of 0.8-1 kHz)
::
