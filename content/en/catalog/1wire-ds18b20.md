#description

Waterproof digital temperature sensor DS18B20.Waterproof, cable length 1, 3, 5 or 10 m. Temperature range -55°C to +125°C accuracy ± 0.5°C (-10°C to +85°C).

- each device has its unique address
- connect up to 30 sensors with Wiren Board controller port
- Convenient replacement of thermistors pt100/pt1000 in some automation tasks
