#description

A learning module with an infrared transmitter. Controls air conditioners, fan coils, TV sets, etc. The module is controlled by a controller or PC over the RS-485 bus with Modbus RTU protocol commands.


#info

## Key features

::product-section{title="General characteristics"}
- Supports a remote digital 1-Wire temperature sensor
- Supports recording complex IR signals
- Ready-made configuration templates for Wiren Board

<br>
::

::product-section{title="Other characteristics"}
- Supply voltage: 9 to 28 VDC
- Interface: RS-485, Modbus RTU
- Small size: 45x35x14 mm
- 3.5 mm jack for connecting the transmitter
- External IR transmitter included
