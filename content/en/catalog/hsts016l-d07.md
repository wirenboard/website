#description

Split-core Hall effect current sensor with 2.5V +/- 0.625V signal, suitable for DC current measurement together with WB-MAI6.


#info

::product-section{title="Technical specifications"}
- Rated current ±100 А.
- Wire outer diameter up to 16 mm
- Dimensions 35\*32\*56 mm
- Weight (not including wires) ~70 g
- Wire length 0.5 m
